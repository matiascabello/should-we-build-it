# User Feedback: Summaries / Long-Note Review

Collected from support tickets, in-app feedback, and user interviews.
Sentiment is mixed on purpose — this is raw, not curated.

## Wants it

- "I take huge meeting notes and never read them again. If the app could just
  tell me the three things I actually need to do, I'd pay more for that." — P2, interview
- "Please add summaries. I have 400 notes and reviewing before a 1:1 is
  painful." — in-app feedback
- "A TL;DR at the top of long notes would be a game changer for my research
  workflow." — support ticket
- "I'd use this every single day for lecture notes." — student user, interview
- "Auto action-items would save me from re-reading everything after standup." — in-app

## Worried about accuracy

- "I tried a summary feature in another app and it confidently made up a
  deadline that didn't exist. I stopped trusting it immediately." — P7, interview
- "If it summarizes wrong and I act on it, that's worse than no summary. How
  will I know when to trust it?" — in-app feedback
- "My notes are messy and half-finished. I doubt an AI could make sense of
  them." — support ticket
- "Would want to see the summary flagged clearly as AI-generated so I don't
  mistake it for something I wrote." — interview

## Wouldn't use it / indifferent

- "My notes are short. I don't need anything summarized." — in-app feedback
- "I organize with headers already, so a summary would be redundant for me." — P4, interview
- "Honestly I'd just turn this off. I like reading my own notes." — in-app
- "Not a priority for me — I care way more about faster search." — support ticket

## Adjacent signal

- "The thing I actually want is better search, not summaries." — recurring theme, ~5 users
- "Could the summary work across a whole folder? One note at a time isn't that
  useful for my project research." — interview (note: out of v1 scope)
- "Does this cost extra? I'm already on the paid tier." — pricing question, several users
